# Credits

- **Sprites:** LuizMelo (itch.io) — Martial Hero, Evil Wizard, Huntress, Monsters Creatures Fantasy, Fire Worm. Released by the author as CC0. Recolored variants made for this game.
- **Sound effects** (`web/sfx/`): taken from the owner's `wallstreet-city` project. The source files there carry Pixabay download names (author and id, for example `dragon-studio-simple-whoosh-382724.mp3`), and the Pixabay Content License allows free commercial use without attribution, though not reselling the sounds on their own. That reading rests on the file names; each file was not checked against its Pixabay page. The boss roars, the boss death groan and the growl (`roar0`, `roar1`, `bossdie`, `growl0`) were made for this game by slowing down and layering grunts and exhales from those same Pixabay-named sources. An earlier roar of unknown origin (`behemoth.mp3` from the `WorldOfLegends` project) is not used.
