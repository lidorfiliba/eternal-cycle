Automation software, bots, changing the game's code or its requests to the server, and exploiting bugs are not allowed.

The server checks every action. Reports that cannot happen in normal play cause an automatic suspension: a day the first time, a week the second, and permanently the third.

Boxes: Some of what credits buy are boxes with random contents. The chances for each kind of item are written next to each box.
