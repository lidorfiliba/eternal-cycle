Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. This is a translation; if it differs from the Hebrew version, the Hebrew version applies.

What is stored

  • Login details: The username you chose. The password and the answer to the recovery question are not stored as they are, only as a one-way encrypted hash from which the original cannot be read.
  • Game data: Characters, gear, progress, party and guild membership, and the character's position on the map while you are connected.
  • Chat messages: The messages you sent in the general chat and the guild chat, with the character's name. Messages are usually deleted within a few days.
  • Private messages: They pass through the server to the other player while both of you are connected, and are not stored.
  • Friends, blocked players and reports: Your lists of friends and blocked players are kept in your account. A report you send about a player is stored with your character's name and reaches the game's admin only.
  • Network address: The network address a sign-up or a failed login came from is kept for a short time, up to a day, to block break-in attempts and mass creation of accounts.
  • Donations: The amount, the time of the request and the identifying detail you wrote so we can match the donation. The transfer itself happens outside the game, and credit card or bank details do not pass through us and are not stored by us.
  • Check log: When the server detects an implausible report, a record of it is kept for dealing with cheating.

Not collected: e-mail address, phone number, credit card details or geographic location. A name appears with us only if you wrote it yourself as the identifying detail of a donation.

Why it is stored

To run the game, keep progress and allow logging in from any device, to show players to each other, and to protect the game from break-ins and cheating. The information is not sold and is not used for advertising.

What other players see

Your character's name, class, level, gear, guild, position on the map and your chat messages are visible to other players. The username you log in with is not shown to them.

What is kept on your device

The browser keeps a login key, so you do not have to type a password every time, and preferences such as sound and language. The game has no tracking or advertising cookies.

Outside providers

  • The database is hosted by Supabase, in a data centre in the European Union.
  • The site's files and the game server are hosted by Render, in a data centre in Germany.
  • The font is loaded from Google Fonts, so your network address is also exposed to Google while the page loads.
  • If you turn on phone notifications, the server stores the notification address your browser created, and the message itself passes through the browser maker's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla). Turning notifications off or deleting the account erases the address.

For how long

Account data is kept as long as the account exists. In addition a daily backup of the data is kept for seven days, so a deleted account disappears from the backups within a week. An account that has been inactive for a long time may be deleted.

Your rights

  • Deletion: On the "Character" screen, under "Account", there is a "Delete account" button. Deleting removes the login details, characters and progress from the server. Old chat messages remain until their routine deletion, with no link to the account.
  • Access and correction: You can ask what information is stored about your account, or have it corrected, through the game's contact page. Do not write a password or a recovery answer there.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13. A parent who finds their child signed up without approval can ask for the account to be deleted.

Security

Access to the database is closed to browsers, and every action goes through the server and is checked there. Still, no system is completely protected. We recommend choosing a password you do not use on other sites.

Changes

This policy may be updated, and the date at the top of the page will change accordingly.
